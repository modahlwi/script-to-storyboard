EXT. STORMY COAST - DAWN

A lone lighthouse on a rocky cliff. Rain hammers the sea.

MARA, 41, weathered, red raincoat, climbs the tower stairs.

CLOSE ON - her hands, raw from years of work.

The sea churns below.

MARA
It's coming.

INT. LAMP ROOM - NIGHT

MARA sets the great rotating lamp in motion.

CLOSE UP - her face, lit amber by the lens.

EXT. OPEN SEA - NIGHT

A small cargo ship lists in the swells.

WIDE ON - the distant lighthouse, barely visible.

MARA (V.O.)
Hold on.

EXT. LIGHTHOUSE - NIGHT

The beam sweeps across the water in a wide cone.

The ship steadies.

MARA watches from the gallery rail.
